Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

🔒

The short version: Tracklytics does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. All your job application data lives on your device and in your personal iCloud account. We have no access to it.

Overview

Tracklytics ("we", "our", or "the app") is a job application tracking app for iOS and macOS. This policy describes what information the app handles, how it handles it, and what choices you have.

We take privacy seriously. The app was designed from the ground up to keep your data in your hands.

Information We Do Not Collect

Tracklytics does not collect, transmit, or have access to:

  • Your job application data (companies, roles, stages, notes)
  • Screenshots or images you import into the app
  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data

There are no analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or third-party data collection tools in this app.

Your Job Application Data

All job application data you enter — companies, roles, application stages, notes, and dates — is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework.

If you have iCloud enabled, this data syncs to your personal iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit framework. This sync is encrypted and managed entirely by Apple. We have no visibility into or access to your iCloud data.

Screenshot & AI Import Feature

When you use the screenshot import feature, the image is analyzed entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework. No screenshot or extracted text is ever transmitted to our servers or any third-party service. The process happens locally on your device, and only the extracted text (company name, job title, etc.) is used to pre-fill the application form.

In-App Purchases

Tracklytics offers optional premium features via in-app purchase. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information — Apple processes all transactions and provides us only with a receipt confirmation.

For details on how Apple handles payment data, please see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app uses iOS's local notification system to send reminders. These are generated entirely on-device and are not routed through our servers.

Third-Party Services

Tracklytics does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The only third-party system the app interacts with is Apple's own infrastructure (iCloud, App Store, Push Notifications).

Data Retention & Deletion

Since we do not collect your data, there is nothing for us to delete. All your data is stored locally on your device and/or your iCloud account. To delete your data:

  • Delete individual applications within the app
  • Delete the app from your device to remove all local data
  • Manage or delete iCloud data via your device's Settings → Apple ID → iCloud

Children's Privacy

Tracklytics is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
